# Code of Conduct

NimPoint follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want NimPoint to be a welcoming project for everyone: customers, agents and contributors, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to crypto.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the people using NimPoint.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information (bank details, wallet keys, phone numbers) without consent.
- Scams, impersonating NimPoint agents, or asking users to pay outside the app.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers by opening a confidential GitHub Security Advisory or contacting the repository owner. Maintainers will review every report and may warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks these rules.
